3 · 小语讲堂 — "的"字短语　page 034

结构助词"的"用在名词、代词、动词、形容词等后面，组成"的"字短语，相当于名词性短语。 The structural particle 的 goes after a noun, pronoun, verb or adjective to make a 的 phrase, which then stands on its own like a noun — "the red one", "the one Dad bought", "the cheap one". The noun it refers to is simply left out.
Hóngsè de, lǜsè de, hēisè de, nǐ xiǎng mǎi nǎge?
红色的、绿色的、黑色的，你想买哪个？
= 红色的书包、绿色的书包、黑色的书包
Zhège miànbāo shì bàba mǎi de, māma mǎi de zài nàr.
这个面包是爸爸买的，妈妈买的在那儿。
= 买的面包
Zhè jiàn yīfu tài guì le, háishi mǎi nà jiàn piányi de ba.
这件衣服太贵了，还是买那件便宜的吧。
= 便宜的衣服

Drag a 的 phrase from the bar into the blank in each sentence — or tap the phrase, then tap the blank. Each phrase is used exactly once. 2 points first try, 1 after a miss.
